The experience

The best view of Lisbon is not in Lisbon.

Overlooking Lisbon is a three-hour guided eBike tour through Almada, on the south bank of the River Tagus. From this side of the river, you can enjoy wide views over Lisbon, the 25 de Abril Bridge and the city’s skyline.

The experience combines Almada’s riverfront, local streets, viewpoints and the area around Cristo Rei. Stops along the route give you time to enjoy the landscape, take photographs and discover a side of the Lisbon region that many visitors miss.

This is a scenic and beginner-friendly experience rather than a demanding off-road ride. The electric assistance reduces the effort required on climbs, while your guide adapts the pace to the group.

You do not need advanced cycling skills or a high level of physical fitness, but you must be able to ride and balance a bicycle independently.

  • Viewpoints around Almada and Cristo Rei.

Before you book

Almada eBike tour FAQ

Practical information about the route, difficulty, equipment, meeting point, weather and booking.

What is the Overlooking Lisbon eBike tour?

Overlooking Lisbon is a three-hour guided electric bike tour through Almada, on the south bank of the River Tagus. The experience combines riverside areas and viewpoints overlooking Lisbon, the 25 de Abril Bridge and Cristo Rei.

Does the tour take place in Lisbon?

No. The tour takes place in Almada, across the River Tagus from Lisbon. This location offers panoramic views over Lisbon’s skyline, the 25 de Abril Bridge and the river.

What places will we see during the tour?

Depending on weather, access and route conditions, the experience may include Almada’s riverfront, viewpoints over Lisbon, the area around Cristo Rei and perspectives over the 25 de Abril Bridge. The exact route is confirmed by the guide.

How long does the tour last?

The experience lasts approximately three hours, including the initial eBike and safety briefing, riding time and stops for sightseeing and photographs.

How much does the tour cost?

The Overlooking Lisbon tour costs €40 per person. The price includes the electric bike, helmet, guide, safety briefing, liability and personal accident insurance and a video recap.

Is the tour suitable for beginners?

Yes. The route is designed to be beginner-friendly and the electric assistance reduces the effort required on climbs. However, every participant must be able to ride and balance a bicycle independently.

Do I need to be physically fit?

You do not need a high level of physical fitness. The eBike provides assistance and the guide adapts the pace to the group. Participants should still be comfortable cycling for approximately three hours with regular stops.

What type of terrain is included?

The tour uses a combination of urban, riverside and viewpoint routes with mixed surfaces. The exact route may change according to weather, access and the experience of the group.

What is included in the tour?

The price includes an all-terrain eBike, helmet, required safety equipment, pre-ride briefing, local guide, liability and personal accident insurance and a video recap of the experience.

What should I wear and bring?

Wear comfortable clothing and closed footwear suitable for cycling. Bring water and, on sunny days, sunscreen and sunglasses.

Is the tour private?

Private groups are available on request, subject to availability. Contact eCoasters before booking so the team can confirm a private departure for your preferred date and number of riders.

Where is the meeting point?

The meeting point is in Almada. The exact location, arrival time and directions are provided after the booking is confirmed. Pick-up or transfer may also be available on request.

In which languages is the tour available?

The guided experience is available in Portuguese, English and Spanish, subject to guide availability.

What happens if the weather is unsafe?

If weather or route conditions make the experience unsafe, eCoasters will contact you to reschedule. If an alternative date cannot be arranged, the amount paid will be refunded according to the applicable booking conditions.

How do I book the tour?

Send an availability request using the form or WhatsApp with your preferred date, time and number of riders. We will confirm availability, the meeting point, final price and payment instructions. Sending the form does not immediately confirm the booking.
