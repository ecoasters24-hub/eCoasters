Questions before booking?

Electric Bike Rental FAQ

How much does it cost to rent an electric bike?

Half-day rental costs €25 per bike. Full-day rates start at €35 per bike and decrease for longer rentals, reaching €22.50 per bike, per day, for rentals of seven days or more.

What is included with the eBike rental?

Every rental includes an all-terrain electric bike, safety helmet, bike lock, phone holder, local delivery and a quick operating and safety briefing.

How far can I ride on one battery?

The estimated battery range is up to 80 km. Actual range depends on the assistance level, terrain, hills, weather conditions and rider weight.

Do I need a driving licence?

No driving licence is required. However, riders should have basic cycling confidence and be able to control a bicycle independently.

Do you deliver the bikes?

Yes. We arrange delivery to hotels, accommodation and agreed meeting points in Costa da Caparica, Almada and surrounding areas. Delivery to Lisbon, Seixal and other locations may be available on request.

Is delivery free?

Free local delivery is included in eligible areas. Send us your address when requesting the rental and we’ll confirm the delivery arrangements before booking.

Can I rent a bike for half a day?

Yes. Half-day electric bike rental is available for €25 per bike, subject to availability.

Are standard bicycles available?

Yes. Standard non-electric bicycles can also be arranged on request.

How do I confirm my booking?

Send us your dates, number of bikes and delivery location. We’ll check availability and contact you by email or WhatsApp with the booking and payment details.

What happens if the weather is unsafe?

If weather conditions make riding unsafe, contact our team so we can review the booking and the available alternatives.
