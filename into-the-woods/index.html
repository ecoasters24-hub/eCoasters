The experience

More than sightseeing. This is an adventure.

Into the Woods is a two-hour guided off-road eBike experience through forest tracks, sandy sections and natural trails near Costa da Caparica.

Unlike a traditional sightseeing tour, the focus is on the ride itself: handling the terrain, moving through the forest and discovering natural areas and coastal viewpoints that are difficult to reach by car.

This is the most technical and physically active of our eBike experiences. Electric assistance helps on climbs, but riders still need enough confidence to control a heavier all-terrain bike on uneven and sometimes loose surfaces.

A local guide accompanies the group throughout the experience, explains how to operate the eBike and adapts the route and pace to the riders, weather and trail conditions.

This experience is not recommended for complete beginners. If you prefer a gentler ride, consider Lisbon’s Hidden Gem .

Rider requirements

Is this experience right for you?

Into the Woods is designed for riders who already feel confident controlling a bicycle. You do not need advanced mountain-bike experience, but you should be comfortable braking, turning and maintaining balance on uneven or loose surfaces.

  • Must be able to ride a bicycle independently
  • Basic cycling confidence and control required
  • Comfortable riding on uneven and sandy surfaces
  • Approximately 1.55 m minimum rider height

Bike fit

Check that the eBike fits you

The all-terrain eBikes used for this experience are heavier than standard bicycles and the saddle height is not adjustable.

They are generally suitable for riders from approximately 1.55 m. Riders around or above 1.90 m may find the bike less comfortable.

Contact us before booking if you are unsure about bike fit. Tell us your height and cycling experience so we can confirm whether this adventure is suitable for you.

Before you book

Into the Woods off-road eBike FAQ

Practical information about difficulty, terrain, bike fit, equipment, weather and booking.

What is the Into the Woods eBike experience?

Into the Woods is a two-hour guided off-road eBike experience through forest tracks, sandy sections and natural trails near Costa da Caparica, approximately 15 minutes from Lisbon.

Is Into the Woods a guided tour?

Yes. A local guide accompanies the group throughout the experience. Unlike a traditional sightseeing tour, the focus is on off-road riding, terrain, nature and adventure rather than historical commentary.

How difficult is Into the Woods?

Into the Woods is an intermediate experience and the most technical of the eCoasters tours. Riders encounter uneven tracks, sandy sections, climbs and descents. The eBike reduces physical effort, but confident bike handling is still required.

Is the experience suitable for beginners?

It is not recommended for complete cycling beginners. You do not need advanced mountain-bike skills, but you must be comfortable braking, turning and maintaining balance on uneven or loose surfaces.

What type of terrain will we ride on?

The route uses forest tracks, natural paths, sandy sections and uneven surfaces. The exact route may change according to weather, trail access and the experience of the group.

How long does the experience last?

The experience lasts approximately two hours, including the initial eBike and safety briefing, riding time and stops during the route.

How much does Into the Woods cost?

Into the Woods costs €40 per person. The price includes the all-terrain eBike, helmet, safety equipment, guide, liability and personal accident insurance and a video recap.

Are there height restrictions?

The bikes are generally suitable for riders from approximately 1.55 m tall. Because the saddle height is not adjustable, riders around or above 1.90 m may find the bike less comfortable. Contact us with your height before booking if you are unsure.

Do I need to be physically fit?

You do not need a high level of fitness because the eBike provides electric assistance. However, this experience is more active than the scenic tours and riders must be able to control the bike on uneven terrain for approximately two hours.

What is included?

The price includes an all-terrain electric bike, helmet, required safety equipment, pre-ride briefing, local guide, liability and personal accident insurance and a video recap.

What should I wear and bring?

Wear comfortable sports clothing and closed footwear with secure grip. Bring water and, on sunny days, sunscreen and sunglasses. We also recommend clothing that can get dusty or dirty.

Is the experience private?

The experience is organised for small groups and may often be private, depending on availability and the booking. Contact eCoasters if you require a guaranteed private experience.

Where is the meeting point?

The meeting area is near Costa da Caparica. The exact location, arrival time and directions are provided after the booking is confirmed. Pick-up or transfer may be available on request.

What happens if the weather is unsafe?

If weather or trail conditions make the experience unsafe, eCoasters will contact you to reschedule. If an alternative date cannot be arranged, the amount paid will be refunded according to the applicable booking conditions.

How do I request a booking?

Send an availability request using the form or WhatsApp with your preferred date, time and number of riders. Include the riders’ approximate heights and cycling experience so that eCoasters can confirm that the bikes and route are suitable.
