Before you book

Questions we get asked

What are the best eBike tours near Lisbon?

eCoasters offers three guided eBike experiences near Lisbon: Into the Woods for forest trails and off-road adventure, Lisbon’s Hidden Gem for the coastline of Costa da Caparica, and Overlooking Lisbon for panoramic views from Almada. The best option depends on your experience level and the type of landscape you want to explore.

Where do the eBike tours take place?

Our guided eBike tours take place in Costa da Caparica and Almada, on Lisbon’s south bank. Depending on the experience, you can explore coastal paths, pine forests, hidden viewpoints, Cristo Rei and panoramic views over Lisbon and the Tagus River.

Are the eBike tours suitable for beginners?

Lisbon’s Hidden Gem and Overlooking Lisbon are beginner-friendly and suitable for riders with basic cycling confidence. Into the Woods is an intermediate experience because it includes forest tracks, sandy sections and uneven terrain. Electric assistance and a safety briefing are provided before every tour.

Can I book a private eBike tour?

Yes. Private eBike tours are available on request, subject to bike and guide availability. Contact us with your preferred date, number of participants and chosen experience, and we will confirm the available options.

What is included in a guided eBike tour?

Every guided tour includes an all-terrain electric bike, helmet, local guide, planned route, safety briefing, liability and personal accident insurance, and a video recap of the experience. Additional arrangements can be confirmed when you make your booking request.

Can I rent an eBike without joining a guided tour?

Yes. You can rent an all-terrain electric bike and explore Costa da Caparica and the surrounding area independently. Rentals are available from half a day to seven days or more and include a helmet, bike lock, phone holder and a quick operating and safety briefing. Delivery and collection conditions depend on the agreed location.
