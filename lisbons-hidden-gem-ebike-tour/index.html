The experience

Discover Costa da Caparica’s hidden coastal landscapes.

Lisbon's Hidden Gem is a three-hour guided eBike tour through the coastal landscapes of Costa da Caparica, in Almada, approximately 15 minutes from Lisbon.

The experience combines coastal scenery, natural trails and viewpoints that many visitors never discover on a conventional visit. There is time to stop, enjoy the landscape and take photographs along the way.

This tour is designed for riders looking for a relaxed combination of nature, sightseeing and light adventure. You do not need advanced cycling skills or a high level of physical fitness, but you must know how to ride and balance a bicycle independently.

Before setting off, your local guide explains how to operate the eBike and checks that everyone feels comfortable. The route and pace can be adjusted to the group, weather and trail conditions.

  • Guided by a local rider who knows the area.
  • Coastal scenery, natural trails and viewpoints.
  • Stops for photographs and to enjoy the landscape.
  • Pace adapted to the least confident rider.
  • No advanced cycling experience required.
  • No driving licence required, subject to confirmation of the bike classification.

Before you book

Costa da Caparica eBike tour FAQ

Practical answers about experience, difficulty, equipment, meeting point, weather and booking.

What is Lisbon's Hidden Gem?

Lisbon's Hidden Gem is a three-hour guided electric bike tour through the coastal landscapes, natural trails and viewpoints of Costa da Caparica, in Almada. It is approximately 15 minutes from Lisbon and combines sightseeing, nature and light adventure.

Where does the eBike tour take place?

The tour takes place in and around Costa da Caparica, on the south bank of the River Tagus. The precise route may change according to weather, trail conditions and the experience of the group.

How long does the tour last?

The experience lasts approximately three hours, including the initial eBike and safety briefing, riding time and stops during the route.

How much does the tour cost?

Lisbon's Hidden Gem costs €40 per person. The price includes the electric bike, helmet, guide, safety briefing, liability and personal accident insurance and a video recap of the experience.

Is the tour suitable for beginners?

Yes. The tour is beginner-friendly and the electric assistance reduces the effort required, particularly on climbs. However, every participant must be able to ride and balance a bicycle independently.

Do I need to be physically fit?

You do not need a high level of physical fitness. The eBike provides electric assistance and the guide adapts the pace to the group. Participants should still be comfortable cycling for approximately three hours with regular stops.

What type of terrain is included?

The experience uses a mixture of paved areas and natural coastal trails. The exact route depends on weather and trail conditions. Contact us before booking if you have concerns about uneven surfaces or mobility.

What is included in the tour?

The price includes an all-terrain eBike, helmet, safety equipment, pre-ride briefing, local guide, liability and personal accident insurance and a video recap of the experience.

What should I wear and bring?

Wear comfortable clothing and closed footwear suitable for cycling. Bring water and, on sunny days, sunscreen and sunglasses. We recommend checking the weather forecast before arriving.

Is the tour private?

Private groups are available on request, subject to availability. Contact us before booking so we can confirm a private departure for your preferred date and number of riders.

Where is the meeting point?

The meeting area is near Costa da Caparica. The exact location, directions and arrival time are provided after the booking is confirmed. Pick-up or transfer may also be available on request.

In which languages is the tour available?

The guided experience is available in Portuguese, English and Spanish, subject to guide availability.

What happens if the weather is unsafe?

If weather or trail conditions make the experience unsafe, we will contact you to reschedule. If an alternative date cannot be arranged, the amount paid will be refunded according to the applicable booking conditions.

How do I book the tour?

Send a booking request using the form or WhatsApp with your preferred date, time and number of riders. We will confirm availability, meeting details, the final price and payment instructions. Submitting the form does not immediately confirm the booking.
